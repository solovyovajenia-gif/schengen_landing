FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you issue Schengen visas?

No. We are a private consultancy providing visa application preparation and guidance services. Schengen visas are issued exclusively by the responsible government authorities.

Can you guarantee visa approval?

No. Approval decisions are made exclusively by the relevant authorities. No private consultant can guarantee visa approval.

Can I apply without using your services?

Yes. You can prepare and submit your Schengen visa application independently through the appropriate official channels.

Which Schengen country should I apply to?

The appropriate country depends on your actual itinerary and applicable Schengen visa rules. We can help you understand the relevant requirements.

How long does the visa process take?

Processing times and appointment availability vary depending on the destination, season and individual circumstances. We cannot guarantee specific timelines.

Are government visa fees included?

Government, consular and authorised visa application centre fees are separate unless expressly included in your service agreement.

Can you help with an appointment?

We can explain the official appointment booking process. Availability is controlled by the relevant official authorities and authorised service providers.
